Privacy built into the journey

Your ID. Protected by design.

Identity verification matters. So does protecting the document you share. SafeCheckInn is designed to support hotel verification while limiting unnecessary exposure of guest ID proof.

✓
Watermarked ID proof

Submitted ID images are watermarked to help discourage unauthorised reuse and make the image's intended check-in purpose clearer.

✓
Limited post-check-in visibility

Once check-in is completed, the ID proof is no longer available for hotel staff to view again through the check-in workflow.

✓
Screen-capture restrictions

Where supported by the app and device, screen-capture restrictions add another layer of protection against casual copying or sharing.

Security works best in layers. Watermarking and access restrictions help reduce misuse, but no digital system can guarantee that information will never be copied or photographed outside its controls.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers about the SafeCheckInn check-in flow.

How do I open the check-in page?

Scan the QR code provided by the participating hotel. It should open the hotel-specific SafeCheckInn check-in page.

Does submitting a request mean my room is confirmed?

Not necessarily. The hotel team reviews the submitted request and handles approval and room assignment.

What if my ID proof image is unclear?

Contact the hotel team for guidance. Staff may ask you to submit a clearer image if the review process requires it.

Who can I contact for help?

Email support.safecheckinn@gmail.com and include a brief description of the issue. Avoid sending sensitive ID documents by email unless the hotel or support team specifically provides a secure process.
