Check-in, made more straightforward

A digital guest check-in journey designed to help guests submit details and hotel staff review them in an organized way.

QR-based access

Scan a participating hotel's QR code to open the relevant check-in page.

Digital details submission

Provide requested check-in information through a web-based form.

ID proof review

Submitted identity proof can be reviewed by authorized hotel staff.

Hotel-specific flow

Each QR link directs guests to the check-in page for the associated hotel.

Clear request status

Requests can move through initiated, approved or rejected statuses.

Staff-assisted completion

Hotel staff handle verification and room assignment according to the hotel's process.

How it works

A simple flow from the guest's phone to the hotel's review process.

01

Scan the hotel QR code

Open the hotel's SafeCheckInn check-in page using the QR code provided by the hotel.

02

Submit the requested details

Complete the form and provide the identity proof requested by the check-in flow.

03

Hotel staff review

Authorized staff review the submission and continue the hotel's approval and room-assignment process.

Designed for guests and hotel teams

SafeCheckInn is being developed to support a more organized digital check-in process for participating hotels, while keeping hotel staff involved in reviewing guest submissions.

• Hotel-specific QR entry points
• Guest details and identity-proof submission
• Staff review of check-in requests
• Approval or rejection within the hotel's workflow
