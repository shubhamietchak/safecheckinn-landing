Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026
Before launch: SafeCheckInn is under development. This policy is a draft of intended practices. The operator must confirm that it accurately describes actual data flows, service providers, retention periods and responsibilities before public launch.

This Privacy Policy explains how SafeCheckInn ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when a guest uses a participating hotel's SafeCheckInn check-in page or contacts us through this website.

1. How SafeCheckInn works

SafeCheckInn provides a digital check-in workflow for participating hotels. A guest may open a hotel-specific check-in page from a QR code, enter requested details and submit identity-proof information for review by authorized hotel staff. The hotel is involved in reviewing submissions and completing its check-in process.

2. Information that may be collected

Depending on the hotel and check-in form, information may include:

  • Guest details such as name, mobile number, email address or address, where requested.
  • Booking and check-in details, including hotel identifier, dates and check-in request status.
  • Identity-proof details or an image of an identity document submitted through the check-in process.
  • Messages and contact details when you email our support address.
  • Basic technical information processed by the website, hosting provider or security systems to deliver and protect the service.

Provide only the information requested by the hotel's check-in form. Do not send payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information through support email.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to create and manage a guest check-in request; allow authorized hotel staff to review details and identity proof; support approval, rejection and room assignment; respond to support enquiries; investigate technical issues; protect the service; and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. How information may be shared

Check-in information may be made available to the participating hotel and its authorized staff as needed to review and process check-in. It may also be processed by service providers that host or operate parts of the service, such as cloud infrastructure and data storage, under the arrangements in place with those providers. We do not intend to sell guest identity information or use identity-proof images for advertising.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to address security, fraud or safety concerns.

5. Identity documents and images

Identity-proof images may contain personal information. They are intended to support the hotel's identity-verification and check-in process. Access should be limited to authorized personnel and service systems that need the information to provide the service.

For questions about a booking or to request access, correction or deletion of submitted information, contact the hotel that handled your check-in and email SafeCheckInn support. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate operational obligations.

6. Storage, security and retention

SafeCheckInn is designed to use hosted systems and access controls to process check-in information. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. The operator must configure and maintain appropriate security measures for the live service.

Information should be retained only as long as needed for the check-in purpose, applicable hotel requirements, dispute handling and legal obligations. Exact retention periods depend on the final service configuration and participating hotel's obligations. Specific periods must be confirmed and added here before public launch.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us with questions about this policy or request help with personal information submitted through SafeCheckInn. For information held by a hotel for its own records, we may direct you to that hotel because it may be responsible for booking and retention decisions. We may need to verify your identity and request details before responding.

8. Children

SafeCheckInn is not designed specifically for children. Where a booking involves a child, the accompanying adult and hotel should follow the hotel's procedures and applicable law.

9. Third-party services and links

The service may rely on hosting, storage or other technical providers, and this website may link to third-party websites. Their own privacy terms may apply to information they process.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SafeCheckInn develops. The effective date at the top of this page will be changed when an updated version is published.

11. Contact SafeCheckInn

For privacy questions, support requests or concerns about this policy, email support.safecheckinn@gmail.com.
